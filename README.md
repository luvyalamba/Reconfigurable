During my internship at the Experiential Learning Centre (ELC), Thapar University, our team developed a prototype for a self-reconfigurable modular robotic platform. The long-term objective of the project was to create autonomous robotic modules capable of mechanically docking with one another to form a larger robotic system, allowing the overall structure to adapt its size, shape, and functionality according to the task at hand. This phase of the project focused on designing, developing, and refining an individual module that would serve as the fundamental building block of the larger system. Through multiple mechanical and electronic design iterations, the prototype evolved into a compact robot capable of three distinct locomotion modes while improving modularity, manufacturability, reliability, and ease of assembly.
